Section 2 · The perpendicular bisector of a chord

A circle is perfectly symmetrical. That symmetry gives us a powerful chord rule.

The big idea

1The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord (cuts it into two equal halves).
2Converse: a line from the centre to the midpoint of a chord is perpendicular to it.
3The perpendicular bisector of any chord passes through the centre.
Pythagoras link: r² = d² + (½ × chord)²

    Section 3 · Angle in a semicircle

    Draw a triangle on a diameter and the angle at the top is always 90°.

    …or drag point P around the circle!

    The big idea

    1If AB is a diameter and P is any other point on the circle, then angle APB = 90°.
    2Converse: if a triangle inscribed in a circle has a 90° angle, the side opposite it is a diameter.
